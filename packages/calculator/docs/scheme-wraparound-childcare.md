### Caveats shown to all users

1. This is paid childcare — providers set their own fees.
2. Tax-Free Childcare or Universal Credit childcare can be used to help with costs.
3. Availability varies by area — speak to the child's school or local Family Information Service.
4. All parents in England have the right to request their school consider setting up wraparound provision.
